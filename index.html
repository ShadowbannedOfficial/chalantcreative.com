Phase 1 — Services

What we actually
do every month.

Five disciplines, run as one operation. No handoffs between the people who plan the post and the people who shoot it.

  1. 01

    Content strategy

    An audit of what exists, a decision about what the account is for, and a calendar that actually ships. Voice, pillars, and posting structure locked before anything goes out.

  2. 02

    Content production

    On-site shoots, photo and video, made for the platform they live on. Reels shot vertical, stills shot for the crop — not repurposed from somewhere else.

  3. 03

    Community management

    DMs and public replies handled on a published schedule, during real hours. Anything that needs a manager, a booking, or a lawyer gets escalated the same day.

  4. 04

    Paid social

    Boost and paid-post setup with audience, budget, and duration chosen on purpose. Ad spend goes straight to the platform — we never mark it up.

  5. 05

    Reporting

    Monthly numbers against the starting baseline: views, viewers, profile visits, link taps, and engagement rate. Then a straight answer — keep, kill, or add.

The first ninety days

Hospitality social rarely proves itself in 30 days. Every engagement starts with a full quarter, run in three moves.

  1. Month one — Foundation

    Audit the profiles. Analyze competitors. Lock voice and visual style. Refresh the bio and highlights. Select the first content batch and shoot it.

  2. Month two — Rhythm

    Full posting cadence. Stories and user-generated content loop running. Posting times adjusted against early performance.

  3. Month three — Proof

    Report against the starting baseline. Then recommend keep, kill, or add — in writing.
